# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Oráculo is committed to a respectful, welcoming and technically rigorous community. Participation is open regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality, disability, appearance, religion or viewpoint expressed in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Give actionable criticism with evidence and context.
- Be explicit about uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent and security boundaries.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Help newcomers understand the project without condescension.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized attention.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Deliberately misleading claims, fabricated evidence or manipulated benchmarks.
- Repeated disruption, personal attacks or bad-faith argument.
- Exploitation of security vulnerabilities against users or infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these standards. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately and focused on community safety.

Until a dedicated private conduct address is published, request a confidential contact channel from the maintainers without including sensitive details in a public Issue. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public. Good-faith disagreement about architecture, evidence or project direction is welcome; hostility toward people is not.
